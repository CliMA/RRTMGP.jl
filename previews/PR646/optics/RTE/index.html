RRTMGP.RTE.NoScatLWRTE — Type
NoScatLWRTE(grid_params::RRTMGPGridParams; params, sfc_emis, inc_flux,
            n_gauss_angles = 1)

A high-level RRTMGP data structure storing the optical properties, sources, boundary conditions and fluxes configurations for a non-scattering longwave simulation.

Fields

  • context: ClimaComms context.
  • op: Optical properties.
  • src: Longwave source functions.
  • bcs: Longwave boundary conditions.
  • fluxb: Temporary storage for bandwise calculations.
  • flux: Longwave fluxes.
  • angle_disc: Angular discretization; n_gauss_angles sets the number of Gauss-Jacobi-5 quadrature angles (1-4) used for the hemispheric integral of the spectral solve. One angle is the diffusivity approximation; more angles resolve the angular integral better, at proportional cost. Gray radiation is single-angle and rejects anything but 1.
  • state_cache: TransposedStateCache with column-first copies of the hot state arrays (refreshed at each spectral solve), or nothing.

G-point groups

A two-stream solve can spread its g-point loop across the threads of one wider kernel instead of running it on one thread per column. These hold the per-group workspaces that make that possible.

RRTMGP.RTE.LWGptGroups — Type
LWGptGroups(grid_params::RRTMGPGridParams, ngroups; params)

Workspaces for solving a column's longwave g-point loop with ngroups threads (lanes of one warp), rather than by one thread's serial loop. ngroups is a power of two up to 32, and a type parameter, so that the lanes' reductions unroll.

Every workspace the solve indexes by column is widened to ncol * ngroups, so a thread's (column, lane) pair selects its slice by one index, except band, which holds what the g-points of one band share in each column: its aerosol and cloud optics and parts of its gas optics (see BAND_PLANES). The fused sweep up keeps in the wide planes what each lane's sweep down needs: the two coefficients of each layer and the albedo and source of each level, of the clear sky in fluxb.flux_up, fluxb.flux_dn, src.albedo and src.src, and of the cloudy sky in op.τ, op.ssa, fluxb.flux_net and src.lev_source. The lanes' cloud masks are bits in shared memory. ngroups > 1 is implemented for CUDA; the CPU driver ignores these workspaces.

source
RRTMGP.RTE.SWGptGroups — Type
SWGptGroups(grid_params::RRTMGPGridParams, ngroups)

The shortwave counterpart of LWGptGroups. Its fused sweep down keeps the clear sky's optics in op and both skies' direct beams in fluxb.flux_dn_dir and fluxb.flux_net; its sweep up then keeps the two coefficients of each layer of the clear sky in op.τ and op.ssa and of the cloudy sky in op.g and the third plane of src.leveldata, and the albedo and source of each level of the clear sky in src.albedo and src.src and of the cloudy sky in fluxb.flux_up and fluxb.flux_dn.

RRTMGP.RTE.default_ngpt_groups — Function
default_ngpt_groups(grid_params::RRTMGPGridParams)

How many g-point groups RRTMGPSolver splits a two-stream solve into when the caller names none: the smallest power of two reaching TARGET_GPT_GROUP_COLUMNS wide columns, capped at MAX_NGPT_GROUPS and at what a GPT_GROUP_MEMORY_DIVISORth of the device's memory affords (see gpt_group_bytes), and one on a CPU.

Grouping changes the order in which a column's g-points are summed, so grouped fluxes differ from a serial solve at roundoff, about 1e-6 relative, and the clear-sky half of a both-skies solve no longer equals a standalone clear-sky solve exactly. Pass ngpt_groups = 1 where bit-for-bit agreement is required.

source
RRTMGP.RTE.gpt_group_bytes — Function
gpt_group_bytes(FT, ncol, nlay, ngroups)

Device bytes that LWGptGroups and SWGptGroups together allocate for ngroups groups of ncol columns on nlay layers, which default_ngpt_groups bounds: the wide planes and surface sources, and the band workspace of each column. The wide optics are over layers but are charged a level each.

source
RRTMGP.RTE.check_band_layout — Function
check_band_layout(lookup)

Check that the gas optics lookup table lookup (a LookUpLW or LookUpSW) fits the band workspace of the fused solves: that the interpolation indices of a layer pack into the bits that the fused kernels give them (4 for the reference temperature, 6 for the pressure, 4 for each η, 7 for the first minor contributor of a band and 4 for their number), and that no band has more minor contributors than BAND_MAX_MINOR in either part of the atmosphere.
